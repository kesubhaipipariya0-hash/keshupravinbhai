About me

YP

Hi, I'm Yatri Pipariya. That's Y-A-T-R-I, and the surname is P-I-P-A-R-I-Y-A.

I live in Surat, Gujarat, the city of diamonds and textiles on the banks of the Tapi river. My family originally belongs to Bhavnagar, a coastal city on the Gulf of Khambhat that is known for Takhteshwar Temple, Nilambag Palace and the famous gathiya.

I'm a student learning web application development. This blog is my final project, built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks and no backend. Blogs you publish here are saved in your own browser using localStorage.
